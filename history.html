— History

From one mandate, to a firm.

The firm was founded in 2014 to administer a single irrevocable trust. What follows is the chronology of how that work became a multi-family fiduciary firm with multi-billion in assets under stewardship across jurisdictions.

  1. 2014

    Foundation

    Finnegan Capital LLC is organised as a Wyoming Series LLC. Liam Finnegan, acting through Liam Finnegan LLC, accepts the firm's first trustee mandate.

  2. 2015

    First Series

    The firm's first protected Series is established to hold a cross-border operating company on behalf of the founding mandate. The per-Series books, banking, and counsel discipline that defines the firm today is set.

  3. 2016

    Second & Third Mandates

    The firm accepts its second and third trustee mandates from referrals of the founding family's counsel. A formal Distribution Committee architecture is adopted across engagements.

  4. 2017

    South Dakota Trust Operations

    A South Dakota trust operations office is opened in Sioux Falls. Henrik Voorhees joins as Chief Trust Officer; the firm formalises its directed-trustee, dynasty, and quiet-trust administrative practice.

  5. 2018

    $1 Billion in Assets Under Stewardship

    The firm crosses $1 billion in assets under stewardship across a small group of family-trust engagements. Margaret Ashford joins as Chief Investment Officer; investment policy is unified across mandates.

  6. 2019

    Family Office Practice

    A dedicated family office practice is established under a single COO function. Catherine Holloway joins to lead operations; consolidated quarterly reporting is standardised across all engagements.

  7. 2020

    London Coordination

    Finnegan Capital (UK) Ltd. is incorporated and a London office is opened in Mayfair to coordinate with European counsel and serve the firm's growing UK and EU engagements.

  8. 2021

    Estate & Tax Practice

    A dedicated Estate & Tax Planning practice is established under Eleanor Pemberton. The firm's engagement template now includes domestic and cross-border succession design as a core service line.

  9. 2022

    $2 Billion in Assets Under Stewardship

    The firm crosses $2 billion in assets under stewardship. Geneva and New York offices are opened; private-credit and real-asset desks are formalised under separate senior partners.

  10. 2023

    Awards & Recognition

    Finnegan Capital is named Trust Company of the Year (Americas) at the STEP Private Client Awards and recognised in the Citywealth Magic Circle for the first time.

  11. 2024

    $3 Billion · Tokyo Office

    The firm crosses $3 billion in assets under stewardship and opens its Tokyo office. Naomi Inoue joins to lead the philanthropy and mission-aligned capital practice. São Paulo office follows in the second half of the year.

  12. 2025

    Singapore · $4 Billion

    A Singapore office is opened to serve South-East Asian families and Variable Capital Company structures. The firm crosses $4 billion in assets under stewardship by year-end and is named Trustee of the Year (Multi-Family) by Trust & Estates Magazine.

  13. 2026

    Today

    Finnegan Capital serves as Trustee, Co-Trustee, or Investment Manager for seven family trusts and family offices, with $4.8 billion+ in assets under stewardship across fourteen jurisdictions and a senior partnership of ten.
