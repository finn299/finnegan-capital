— Structure

One parent. Many cells. One fiduciary above them all.

The architecture by which the Finnegan Capital family enterprise is held. Each internal structure is administered inside its own protected Series, with the South Dakota Domestic Business Trust held beneath the parent.

  1. Layer i. Principal

    Liam Finnegan

    Acting through Liam Finnegan LLC, a New Mexico Limited Liability Company

    The top of the structure is the principal of the family enterprise, acting through Liam Finnegan LLC as Manager. All authority within the structure flows from this seat, and is bounded by the governing instruments of the entities below.

  2. Layer ii. Parent Vehicle

    Finnegan Capital LLC

    Wyoming Series LLC · Private family-enterprise vehicle

    The fiduciary at the parent. Acts as Trustee of the family enterprise's South Dakota Domestic Business Trust and as the governing parent of the Series LLC architecture. The firm is not a chartered trust company, is not a registered investment adviser, and offers no services to outside parties.

  3. Layer iii. Domestic Business Trust

    Westmere Heritage Trust

    SD Domestic Business Trust under SDCL Chapter 47-14A · Business ID DT313449

    The family enterprise's South Dakota Domestic Business Trust, with Finnegan Capital LLC as Trustee. Use of the word "Trust" in the name is approved in writing by the SD Division of Banking. The trust is registered with the SD Secretary of State and is held internally to the enterprise.

  4. Layer iv. Per-Structure Series

    Protected cells beneath the parent.

    Each internal structure within its own Series · separate books, banking, and counsel

    Distinct internal structures within the family enterprise are administered through dedicated protected Series of the parent. Each Series carries its own ledgers, its own bank accounts where applicable, its own EIN where appropriate, and its own counsel of record. Inter-Series transactions are documented with formal authorisation and an audit trail.

    The structure is designed for clarity within the enterprise, not for outside parties. Specific Series identities, counterparties, and arrangements are not disclosed publicly.
