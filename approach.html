— 02 / Approach

Three commitments. One charter.

Stewardship is not a slogan. It is the daily test against which every decision within the family enterprise is weighed.

  1. Preserve what was built.

    Capital held within the enterprise is treated as held for the long term. Drawdowns are an exception, not a tool. The default answer to a discretionary action is a question — about purpose, about timing, about the next thirty years.

  2. Deploy with conviction.

    Concentration where the thesis is earned; diversification where it is not. We will not hold what we cannot defend in writing to a future trustee of the enterprise. Decisions are slow on purpose.

  3. Account without exception.

    Each Series within the parent carries its own books, its own audit trail, and its own counsel of record. Internal legibility is non-negotiable; external privacy is. The two are the same idea, addressed to different audiences.

“

We are not in the business of being interesting. We are in the business of being still here, holding the structure together, when the next generation arrives.

— From the firm's charter memorandum
