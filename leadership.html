— Leadership

A single point of authority.

Finnegan Capital LLC is administered by a single principal, supported by independent professional advisors retained on a project basis. The firm has no employees, no partnership, and no public bench.

Principal

Liam Finnegan

Acting through Liam Finnegan LLC, a New Mexico Limited Liability Company.

Liam Finnegan is the principal of Finnegan Capital LLC. Acting through Liam Finnegan LLC as Manager, he directs the parent vehicle's governance, structure, and the administration of the internal mandates held within the family enterprise.

The firm's work centres on long-horizon capital — its preservation, its productive deployment within the family enterprise, and the architectures required to keep both legible across time.

The principal maintains a deliberately narrow public profile. The firm does not solicit, accept, or service business from any person or entity outside its affiliated group.

— Outside Professionals

Counsel, accountants, and custodians, engaged independently.

Specialist functions — legal counsel, tax and accounting, custody, and audit — are performed by independent licensed professionals and firms engaged on a project basis. None are partners, members, or employees of Finnegan Capital LLC, and none are held out as representing the firm.

Identities of outside professionals are not disclosed publicly. The firm does not maintain a published roster, a partnership, or a senior bench. References on this website to officers, partners, employees, or functional leads should not be inferred as identifying any specific natural person.
