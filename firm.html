— 01 / The Firm

A private multi-family trustee and investment firm.

Finnegan Capital LLC serves as Trustee — and, in select engagements, as Investment Manager — to a discreet group of family trusts and family offices. Multi-billion in assets are stewarded across jurisdictions under one fiduciary standard.

— Charter

Wyoming Series LLC.
One firm. Many cells.

A private firm operating under a deliberately quiet mandate: to preserve, deploy, and account for capital across generations on behalf of the families and offices in our care.

Finnegan Capital LLC is organised as a Wyoming Series LLC. The Series chassis lets us hold each client engagement inside its own protected cell — separate books, separate banking, separate counsel — while preserving a single fiduciary brain at the parent. The result is precision without sprawl, and continuity across decades rather than quarters.

The firm acts as Trustee, Co-Trustee, or Investment Manager depending on the instrument and the family's preference. Around that core seat sits a full suite of family-office disciplines: estate and succession design, trust administration, tax planning, philanthropy, custody architecture, and consolidated reporting.

Engagements are long-dated and accepted by reference. We do not market, do not raise outside capital, and do not solicit. The firm is built to keep doing the same work, well, for the next set of beneficiaries who have not yet arrived.
