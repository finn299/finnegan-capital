— Areas of work

Categories, not engagements.

The categories below describe the kinds of work conducted within the family enterprise and its affiliated structures. They are not engagements, are not offered to outside parties, and should not be construed as a service menu.

Trust Administration

Domestic Business Trust

Administration of the South Dakota Domestic Business Trust registered under SDCL Chapter 47-14A, for which Finnegan Capital LLC serves as Trustee. The trust holds and administers assets internally to the family enterprise, in accordance with the governing instrument and the written charter adopted for the structure.

Parent Governance

Wyoming Series LLC

Governance of the Wyoming Series LLC parent under a single fiduciary at the parent and a written charter for each cell. Per-Series books, per-Series banking where appropriate, per-Series counsel of record, and inter-Series transactions documented on the audit trail.

Internal Stewardship

Long-Horizon Asset Holding

Stewardship of the family enterprise's long-horizon assets — held for preservation and productive deployment within the affiliated group, rather than for trading, redemption, or third-party return. Investment policy is internal; no outside capital is accepted.

Coordination

Counsel & Counterparty Coordination

Engagement of outside counsel, accountants, custodians, and operating managers on a project basis to discharge specialist work where the family enterprise requires it. None of these professionals are employees, partners, or members of Finnegan Capital LLC.

The descriptions above are categorical. No engagement, transaction, counterparty, or figure is described, and none should be inferred. The firm does not solicit, accept, or service business from any person or entity outside its own affiliated group.
